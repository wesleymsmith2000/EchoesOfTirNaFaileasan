# Lore Boundaries

- Tir Na Faileasan is the AI GM's diegetic voice, not authoritative state.
- The simulation determines what can happen.
- Tir Na Faileasan determines how the world notices, interprets, responds, and remembers.
- Humanity is visiting the road, not conquering it.
- Hospitality, mercy, protection, and reconciliation must remain mechanically meaningful.
